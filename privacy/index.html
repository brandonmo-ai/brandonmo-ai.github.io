Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 24, 2026

Mason Davis Personal Training ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you contact us or use our website (trainermasonoc.com) and our advertising, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you book a free assessment or contact us — on our website or through a Facebook/Instagram lead form — we collect your name, email, phone number, and any details you share about your goals.
  • Information collected automatically. When you visit our website, we and our providers may collect usage data such as your device, browser, pages viewed, and general location through cookies and similar technologies, including the Meta (Facebook) Pixel.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and schedule your free assessment or training.
  • To provide, personalize, and improve our coaching and services.
  • To contact you about your training and, where permitted, send relevant offers. You can opt out of marketing messages at any time.
  • To measure and improve our advertising on Meta platforms.

How your information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us operate — such as Meta (Facebook/Instagram) for lead forms and advertising, and scheduling or email tools — and only as needed to provide our services, or where required by law.

Advertising & analytics

We use the Meta Pixel and similar tools to understand ad performance and show relevant ads. You can control ad personalization in your Facebook ad settings and through your browser's cookie controls.

Data retention

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, then delete or anonymize it.

Your rights

You may request to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or opt out of marketing, by emailing us at masondavis4@yahoo.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

Our services and website are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to this policy
